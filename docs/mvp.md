# Profitability MVP

Build one complete learning path that takes a student from calculating profit and margin to explaining why a retailer is losing money.

## Learning path

| Step | Learning objective | Estimated time |
| --- | --- | --- |
| How businesses make money | Identify revenue, costs, and profit in a simple business | 3 min |
| Calculate profit and margins | Calculate profit and profit margin from an exhibit | 5 min |
| Understand revenue drivers | Explain changes in revenue using price and volume | 5 min |
| Identify rising costs | Find the costs contributing most to a profit decline | 6 min |
| Interpret financial exhibits | Use a table or chart to support a conclusion | 7 min |
| Build profitability issue trees | Organize possible causes into a structure for investigation | 8 min |
| Why is the retailer losing money? | Diagnose the main driver and recommend an action backed by evidence | 20 min |

Plan for 5–10 validated exercises across the path. Use a mix of calculations, exhibit interpretation, and structured reasoning so the final case tests skills the learner has already practiced.

## Final case

Give the learner a clear client objective and access to the exhibits they need to investigate. The solver should let them review those exhibits, work through staged questions, and submit a short recommendation. Keep calculations and notes close to the evidence with a calculator and scratchpad. After submission, show the expected calculations and an example recommendation with its supporting evidence.

## Release criteria

- A student can start at the first lesson and reach the final case through one continuous learning path.
- Every exercise includes an answer key or rubric and an explanation. Numerical questions specify units and any accepted rounding or range.
- The final case can be solved using its supplied exhibits. The recommendation rubric credits defensible conclusions supported by those exhibits.
- Experienced consulting applicants and former consultants review the material for accuracy, clarity, and realism.
- Student playtests show whether learners understand the flow, interpret the feedback, and choose to complete the path.

Accounts, the broader practice library, rankings, and subscriptions sit in [later phases](roadmap.md).
