# Product overview

Caselevel starts with university students preparing for management consulting internships and entry-level roles. It gives them a way to build consulting skills independently, from their first profitability calculation to a full client recommendation.

## How learners improve

1. **Learn.** Build skills through short interactive lessons. A lesson introduces a concept and asks the learner to apply it.
2. **Practice.** Work through standalone problems at increasing difficulty and apply multiple skills in a full business case.
3. **Review.** See which calculations, assumptions, or conclusions need work. Use the explanation to improve on the next attempt.
4. **Compete.** Later, tackle standardized challenges that reward accuracy first and use speed as a secondary factor.

The initial curriculum is planned around profitability, mental math, structured thinking, and market sizing. The [MVP](mvp.md) begins with profitability. Future paths can cover business fundamentals, market entry, growth strategy, pricing, operations, M&A, data interpretation, and case communication.

## How feedback earns trust

- Use deterministic grading for objective questions and accepted ranges for estimates where appropriate.
- Use explicit rubrics for open-ended answers. Give credit for multiple defensible solutions and tie feedback to the learner’s reasoning and the evidence in the case.
- Treat AI as support for hints, explanations, and rubric-based feedback. Learners should be able to see why their answer received the feedback it did.
- Keep XP and mastery separate. XP rewards participation. Mastery reflects demonstrated performance at meaningful difficulty, so repeated easy answers have limited value as evidence of mastery.

## Product principles

- Make independent practice valuable enough to use regularly.
- Prioritize a small library of accurate, realistic, reviewed exercises.
- Keep short lessons and daily drills mobile-friendly. Give full cases room to breathe on desktop.
- Use goals, streaks, and milestones to encourage practice. Keep practice available regardless of streaks or activity.
- Focus on consulting preparation until learners find it valuable enough to return on their own.
