# Roadmap

Each phase depends on what students do with the previous release. The phases describe planned scope and evidence to look for before expanding.

| Phase | Planned scope | What to validate |
| --- | --- | --- |
| MVP | One profitability path, 5–10 validated exercises, interactive case solver, and basic feedback | Students understand the experience and voluntarily complete the path |
| Alpha | 25–40 problems, accounts, skill progress, a dashboard, and a filterable practice library | Students return on their own and can tell what to practice next |
| Beta | More curriculum, weekly competitions, university groups, and improved evaluation | Students return, trust the feedback, and share challenges |
| Launch | Expanded library, Pro subscription, polished onboarding, and product analytics | Students keep practicing and show willingness to pay |
| Future | Simulated case interviews, reviewed community contributions, club subscriptions, and advanced competition formats | Each addition addresses demonstrated demand |

## Longer-term direction

- **Onboarding and discovery.** Use goals and a short diagnostic to suggest a starting point. Let learners find problems by skill, difficulty, time, and completion status.
- **Daily practice.** Offer a free daily challenge and show progress, skill strengths, and the next suggested exercise on the dashboard.
- **Competition and community.** Start with standardized challenges. Explore university leaderboards, club groups, and discussion threads as participation grows.
- **Paid plans.** Keep introductory learning and daily practice available in the free tier. Explore Pro for advanced cases, deeper feedback, and personalized training. Keep paid benefits separate from competitive scoring.
