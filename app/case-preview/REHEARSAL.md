# Landing page visual rehearsal

The hero uses the Stageshow static-card composition with a fully framed desktop window and a compact mobile card. Soft, short shadows stay within the stage. The profitability card sits outside the text column.

The curriculum visual uses the cursorless Stageshow payout-flow reference. On entry, the Stage draws a connector from profit and branches it to revenue and costs. Stageshow States reveals the two formulas, then the diagram holds. Reduced-motion settings show the complete diagram immediately.

Desktop and phone captures at 1440 and 390 px passed the Stageshow rehearsal gate. The animated captures had short sampling gaps, so the independent review judged the frames captured. Browser interaction checks on the published route verified that all connectors and formulas appear at both widths.
